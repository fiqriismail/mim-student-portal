Postgraduate Diploma in Digital Marketing

9 months · On-campus and online sessions

About this course

This programme covers digital channel strategy, performance marketing, analytics and brand management, with a project-based capstone in the final term.

Sessions combine on-campus workshops with online lectures, designed for working professionals balancing study alongside a job.

Entry requirements

A bachelor's degree in any discipline, or an equivalent professional qualification with at least two years of relevant work experience.

Fees

LKR 285,000, payable in three instalments. A detailed fee schedule is provided at enrolment.
